How it works

Three steps. One command.

From describing your offer to receiving scored, prioritized leads in your inbox.

1

Describe your offer

Tell it what you sell and where. For example: "I sell luxury saunas to hotels in Spain". Claude AI designs your verticals, cities, keywords, pitch angle, and scoring rubric automatically.

2

Scrape & score

The scraper hits Google Places for each city. For every business found, it checks the website, analyzes Google Places types, and scores against your custom rubric into A+, A, B, or C tiers.

3

Export & outreach

Priority leads are exported to CSV, synced to Notion CRM, and cold emails are auto-generated with your pitch angle. Control everything via Telegram bot or CLI.
